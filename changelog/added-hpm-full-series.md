Added HPMicro HPM6700/HPM6300/HPM6200/HPM6800/HPM6E00/HPM6P00/HPM5E00/HPM5100 target support, including dual-core hart definitions and XPI NOR flash algorithms. HPM5300/6P00/5E00 use a small hpm53x1-flavor algorithm on XPI0 0xF3000000; HPM5100 uses the same flavor on XPI0 0xF00E0000; HPM5300 was rebuilt with freq_opt 0x5. External NOR, 4 MiB SiP, XPI1 and the HPM5E00 hybrid use the generic ROM-API C loader.
